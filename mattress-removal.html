Got a question about mattress removal? You’re not the first.

Everything you were about to Google answered here.

01 Do you take the box spring and frame too? +
Yes. Mattress, box spring, bed frame, headboard and slats all go on the same trip. It is priced as one job by the space it fills, not per piece.
02 Can you get it down a narrow staircase? +
That is most of these jobs. Two people, one at each end, round the landing and out. If a doorway is genuinely too tight we will tell you on the walkthrough rather than after we have marked the wall.
03 Do I need to bag or wrap it first? +
No. Leave the bedding on if you want. Nothing needs stripping, bagging or dragging to the curb before the crew arrives.
04 What happens to the mattress afterward? +
Anything still usable is donated or resold first. Mattress recycling depends on what the local facility will take, so the honest answer is that some go to the landfill, but it is the last stop rather than the first.
05 How much does mattress removal cost? +
By volume, like everything else. A single mattress and box spring is usually a quarter load, around $150. A full bedroom set with the frame runs closer to a half. You get a firm number before anything moves.
06 Do I need to be home? +
Someone needs to let the crew in and point at what is going. After that you do not have to hover. Plenty of these are booked by a landlord or a family member who is not on site.
