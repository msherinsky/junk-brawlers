The Bulk Haul Day Came and Went. The Pile Didn’t.

Forsyth has been building for twenty years straight. Two things pile up behind that, and neither one waits for a scheduled pickup.

What the Bulk Haul Day Leaves at the Curb

Most of the big communities out here run a bulk pickup once or twice a year. Word goes out, everybody drags something down to the curb the night before, and the truck comes through in the morning.

It takes the easy half. Cardboard, a bookshelf, bagged yard waste. What it usually will not take is a mattress, a fridge with the compressor still in it, a hot tub cover, or the stack a contractor left in the side yard. Those are the piles still sitting out on Monday afternoon, and by then the whole street has watched them sit.

We do not work from a list of what qualifies. If it is on the driveway it goes on the truck, and if it is still in the basement we go down and get it. You never have to drag anything out first, which is the part that actually costs you a Saturday. And we come the day you call instead of the day the calendar says.
