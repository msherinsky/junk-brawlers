Pool Removal Done Right

Above-ground pools are a major undertaking to remove. The liner, the wall panels, the frame, the filter system, the decking — it all has to come apart in the right order and get hauled away in pieces. Most homeowners don't have the crew or the truck to handle it themselves.

Junk Brawlers comes prepared. Tony and his crew dismantle your above-ground pool systematically, remove all components, and haul everything away — including the liner, the frame, and any associated equipment. We leave your yard ready for grass seed or whatever you have planned next.

Need the hot tub gone too? Ask us about combining services for better value. Tony handles both, and many North Georgia homeowners have cleared their entire backyard in a single visit.

Pool Removal FAQ

How much does above-ground pool removal cost in North Georgia? +
Most above-ground pool removals in North Georgia run $250–$600 depending on pool size, condition, and whether a deck is attached. Junk Brawlers provides a free, no-obligation on-site quote before any work begins. Call (404) 632-9165 for a fast estimate.
Do you remove in-ground pools? +
Junk Brawlers specializes in above-ground pool removal. In-ground pool removal is a full demolition and excavation project that requires heavy equipment. We can refer you to qualified contractors in North Georgia if that's what you need.
Should I drain the pool before you arrive? +
Yes — please drain the pool before our arrival. This makes the job faster and keeps costs down. If you're unsure how to drain it properly, let us know when you schedule and we'll walk you through it.
Can you also remove the deck around the pool? +
Yes. Junk Brawlers handles deck removal in addition to pool removal. If you have a wooden deck surrounding or attached to the pool, we can remove both in the same visit. Ask about bundled pricing when you call.
