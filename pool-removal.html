The Pool Hasn’t Been Used in Three Years. The Yard Has.

Above-ground pools don’t age gracefully. Liners tear, frames rust, pumps fail, and what was once the centerpiece of the backyard becomes the thing everyone works around. You stop maintaining it, then you stop looking at it, and then your real estate agent tells you it needs to go before you list.

The problem isn’t deciding to remove it — it’s figuring out who will actually do it. Most junk haulers won’t touch a pool. The ones that do often expect you to drain it first, disassemble it yourself, and have it ready to load. That’s not a removal service. That’s a haul.

Tony handles the whole job. You don’t touch a thing.

No need to drain before he arrives. Tony drains the pool on-site as part of the job, then breaks down the frame, liner, walls, and all equipment.

Steel and aluminum components sorted for recycling — not dumped. What can’t be recycled is disposed of responsibly.

Pre-sale, storm-damaged, or just done with it — Tony quotes it after seeing it and hauls it the same day or next day. One call.

Here’s how the job goes from call to clear.

Drained. Broken Down. Hauled. Yard Clear.

1

Drain & Disconnect

Tony arrives and drains the pool on-site — no prep work needed on your end. Once drained, he disconnects the pump, filter, hoses, and any electrical connections. He assesses the full scope of the job and confirms the price before anything is touched. Call (404) 632-9165 to get a quote — Tony will ask a few questions about your pool and give you a number before he shows up.

2

Break It Down

Once drained, Tony disassembles the pool completely — frame walls, top rails, liner, uprights, and all equipment. Steel and aluminum components are separated for recycling rather than going straight to the landfill. Storm-damaged or partially collapsed pools are handled the same way — often easier to break down than intact ones.

3

Hauled & Gone

Everything loads onto the truck — liner, frame, pump, filter, hoses, and anything else that came with the pool. The yard is left clear. No pile of scrap metal sitting in the corner, no liner draped over the fence. Tony hauls it all in one trip for most standard above-ground pools. Larger pools or pools with extensive equipment may take two loads — he quotes per trip upfront so you know before he starts.

Common questions about above-ground pool removal.

Answered before you have to ask.

01 Do you remove in-ground pools or only above-ground? +
Tony removes above-ground pools only — the full job, including draining, disassembly, and haul. In-ground pool removal is a different kind of excavation work that he doesn’t offer. If you have an above-ground pool, call (404) 632-9165 and Tony can give you a quote.
02 Do I need to drain the pool before you arrive? +
No. Tony drains the pool on-site as part of the job. You don’t need to do anything before he arrives. Just call (404) 632-9165 and he’ll handle everything from start to finish.
03 What happens to the liner, frame, and equipment? +
Everything comes with Tony — liner, frame walls, top rails, pump, filter, hoses, and any other equipment. Steel and aluminum components are sorted for recycling rather than going straight to the landfill. What can’t be recycled is disposed of responsibly.
04 How long does pool removal take? +
Most above-ground pool removals take half a day to a full day depending on pool size, water volume, and how much equipment is involved. Tony gives you a realistic time estimate when he quotes the job. Call (404) 632-9165 for details.
05 Do you remove the concrete pad or base underneath the pool? +
Call Tony directly at (404) 632-9165 to discuss your specific situation. Concrete removal depends on the size and thickness of the slab, and he’ll give you an honest answer about what he can do after understanding what you have.
06 My pool is collapsed or storm-damaged — can you still remove it? +
Yes. Tony handles collapsed and storm-damaged above-ground pools. A damaged pool is often easier to break down than an intact one. Call (404) 632-9165 and describe what you have — Tony will tell you what the job involves and what it costs.
