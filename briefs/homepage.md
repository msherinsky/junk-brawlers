## Color Doctrine

**Container:** Near-black #0D0010
The ambient room — every section background. Creates depth and seriousness that makes the purple and orange pop. In a market of GoDaddy one-pagers and SaaS builder templates, the near-black says "this is a real brand." Never competes for attention; it is the space where everything else reads.

**Heat:** Orange #FF6B00 — sourced from the ninja's eye glow in the logo.
The action color. When orange appears, something matters: a CTA button, a review badge star, a stat number, a pull quote border. Appears at every scroll position so the page feels continuous. Never decorative. Only on: CTA buttons, star badge fills, stat numbers in The Owner section, pull quote left-borders in The Work and Owner sections, step numbers in Process, question markers in FAQ.

**Voice:** White — headlines and all body copy on dark backgrounds.
Its only role is to deliver the message. Never as a decorative element.

**Purple accent #8000FF:** Not a container or heat element — it's the brand identity layer between them. Used for section borders, card highlights, trust-bar backgrounds, and the horizontal rules that separate sections. It signals "this is Junk Brawlers" without competing with orange for the eye. Do not use purple on CTAs or stat highlights — that belongs to orange.
