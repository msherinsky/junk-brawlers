There Is No Curbside Here. It Is All on You to Move.

White County does not run a garbage truck past your house. Whatever you are finished with, you are the one who gets it to Hulsey Road, and only some of it is allowed in when you arrive.

Everything Has to Fit in a Thirty-Three Gallon Bag

The Convenience and Recycling Center on Hulsey Road takes household garbage bagged, and the bag cannot be bigger than thirty-three gallons. That rule is fine for kitchen trash. It is the reason the rest of it never leaves.

A chest freezer does not go in a bag. Neither does a mattress, a box spring, a recliner, a bed frame, or the cabinets that came out of a kitchen. Anything over the limit becomes your own problem to move, which means your own truck, your own back, and however many trips it takes.

So it goes in the barn instead. Or the carport, or the spare room, and it sits there being the thing you keep meaning to deal with. We take the whole lot in one visit, out of wherever it ended up, and you do not carry any of it.

Shut on Monday, Thursday and Sunday

The center is open Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday, plus Saturday morning. It is closed Monday, Thursday and all day Sunday, and what it turns away goes to the transfer station instead, which is a second trip to a second place.

Cleveland is the gateway to Helen, so a good share of the property here is somebody’s weekend. You drive up Friday evening, you leave Sunday afternoon, and the one full day you are standing in the house is a day the county is shut. The hot tub cover, the deck furniture and the old grill are still there the next time you come up.

We work around your visit instead of the county’s calendar. Call in the morning and same day is usually on the table, Sunday included, whether that is a cabin on the Yonah Mountain roads, a rental changing over, or a longtime family place near the square that has to be emptied properly.
