1. Services

Junk Brawlers LLC ("Junk Brawlers," "we," "us") provides junk removal and related services in North Georgia. All services are subject to a pre-job assessment and pricing agreement before work begins.

2. Quotes and Pricing

All prices are provided as free, no-obligation quotes. Final pricing is confirmed before any work begins. Prices are based on volume and type of material. There are no hidden fees — the price you're quoted is the price you pay, barring significant undisclosed additional items.

3. Prohibited Items

We do not haul hazardous materials, chemicals, asbestos, biohazardous waste, medical waste, propane tanks (full), or any material requiring a hazmat license to transport. Customers are responsible for disclosing the nature of materials to be removed prior to service.

4. Payment

Payment is due upon completion of service. We accept cash, credit cards, Venmo, and Zelle. No work begins until a price is agreed upon by both parties.

We ask for reasonable notice if you need to cancel or reschedule an appointment. No cancellation fees are charged.
