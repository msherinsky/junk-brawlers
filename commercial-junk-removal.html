Common questions about commercial junk removal.

Answered before you have to ask.

01 Can you work outside normal business hours? +
Yes. Tony regularly schedules early morning, after-hours, and weekend jobs for businesses that can’t have a junk crew walking through during operating hours. Call (404) 632-9165 to discuss your schedule and Tony will tell you what he can do.
02 Do you work with property managers for between-tenant cleanouts? +
Yes. Between-tenant cleanouts are one of Tony’s most common commercial jobs. He understands the timeline pressure — the next tenant’s lease has a start date and the unit needs to be clear. Call (404) 632-9165 and Tony can often schedule same-day or next-day for property management situations.
03 Can you remove office furniture, cubicles, and equipment? +
Yes. Office desks, chairs, cubicle panels, filing cabinets, shelving — Tony hauls it all. For large office cleanouts with a lot of heavy furniture, he gives you a per-load estimate after walking the space so you know exactly what the full job will cost. Call (404) 632-9165 for a free quote.
04 How is working with Tony different from a national junk removal franchise? +
With a national franchise, you don’t know who’s showing up — it’s whoever is on the truck that day. With Tony, it’s the same person who answered your call, walked the space for the quote, and will be there for every load. For commercial and property management clients, that consistency matters. You know who has access to your property.
05 Do you do recurring commercial cleanouts or is it one-time only? +
Both. Tony does one-time cleanouts and also works with property managers and businesses that have regular hauling needs. If you have ongoing volume, call (404) 632-9165 and Tony can talk through what a recurring arrangement would look like for your situation.
06 Can you handle confidential document disposal? +
Tony hauls paper and document boxes but does not provide certified shredding or chain-of-custody destruction. If your documents require certified destruction for compliance purposes, you’ll need a dedicated document shredding service for that portion. For everything else — furniture, equipment, general office material — call (404) 632-9165.
