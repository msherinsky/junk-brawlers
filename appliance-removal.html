Appliance Removal FAQ

How much does appliance removal cost in North Georgia?

Most single appliance pickups in North Georgia run between $75 and $150. Multiple appliances or a full kitchen and laundry haul typically run $200 to $400. The final price depends on the number of items, how difficult the access is, and whether refrigerant handling is needed. Tony gives a clear price on-site before anything is touched. No hidden fees and no obligation to book.

Do I need to disconnect the appliance before you arrive?

Refrigerators should be emptied and defrosted before the crew arrives. For electric appliances, Tony and his crew handle the disconnect. Gas appliances must be disconnected and capped by a licensed plumber before arrival. Junk Brawlers does not work gas lines. Call (404) 632-9165 if you need a referral.

Does Junk Brawlers remove appliances from upstairs or tight spaces?

Yes. Tony and his crew bring the right equipment for stairs, tight hallways, and awkward access points in North Georgia homes. They protect floors and walls during the move. Mention the access situation when you book so the crew arrives prepared.

Does Junk Brawlers handle refrigerators that still have Freon?

Yes. Under EPA Section 608, refrigerant-containing units must be handled by certified recyclers. Tony routes all fridges, freezers, window ACs, and dehumidifiers through EPA-compliant disposal in North Georgia. He does not dump refrigerant-containing appliances. Using a professional for appliance removal in North Georgia protects you from fines and confirms the job is done correctly.

Can you remove just one appliance or is there a minimum load?

Single-appliance pickups are fine. Tony regularly comes out for a single refrigerator or washer anywhere in North Georgia. Pricing is volume-based. If you have multiple appliances, the per-item cost goes down. There is no minimum load requirement.

What happens to my old appliances after removal?

Tony tries to sell working appliances locally first on Facebook Marketplace so they stay in the North Georgia community. A working fridge or washer that someone in Canton or Cumming can use for $50 is a better outcome than sending it to a facility. Tony donates what cannot be sold. Tony recycles what cannot be donated. Fridges, freezers, and ACs go through EPA-certified recyclers for refrigerant handling. Only what genuinely cannot be reused or recycled goes to a landfill. The landfill is the last stop, not the first.

Does Junk Brawlers offer same-day appliance removal in North Georgia?

Often, yes. Tony operates 7 days a week across North Georgia and typically responds within minutes. Same-day and next-day appliance removal pickups are common depending on the schedule. Call (404) 632-9165. When a time is confirmed, Tony sends a text when the crew is on the way.
