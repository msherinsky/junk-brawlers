Appliance Removal FAQ

How much does appliance removal cost in North Georgia? +
Single appliance pickups typically start around $75�$150. Multiple appliances in the same visit cost less per unit. Junk Brawlers always provides a free on-site quote before work begins. Call (404) 632-9165 for fast pricing.
Do I need to disconnect the appliance before you arrive? +
For refrigerators, please empty and defrost before we arrive. For electric appliances, we can disconnect safely. Gas appliances should be disconnected by a licensed plumber or gas tech before our arrival � we don't handle gas line work. Call us if you need a referral.
Do you remove appliances from upstairs or tight spaces? +
Yes. Tony and his crew are experienced at navigating stairs, tight hallways, and awkward access points. We use proper equipment and take precautions to protect floors and walls. Just let us know the situation when you book so we can come prepared.
What happens to my old appliances after removal? +
We properly recycle refrigerants from fridges and freezers (required by EPA regulations). Working appliances may be donated to local organizations. Metal components are recycled rather than landfilled when possible. Our goal is to keep as much out of the dump as we can.
Do you handle refrigerators that still have Freon? +
Yes. Refrigerant-containing units (refrigerators, freezers, window ACs, dehumidifiers) require certified handling under EPA Section 608. Junk Brawlers disposes of these through certified recyclers — we don't just dump refrigerant-containing appliances. This is one of the key reasons to use a professional rather than hauling it yourself.
Can you remove just one appliance, or do you require a minimum load? +
Single-appliance pickups are fine. Tony regularly comes out for a single refrigerator or washer. Pricing is volume-based — if you have multiple appliances or want to add other items to the same trip, the per-item cost goes down. There's no minimum load requirement.
Do you offer same-day appliance removal in North Georgia? +
Often, yes. Same-day availability depends on schedule, but Tony serves the Dawsonville area and frequently offers same-day or next-day pickups across North Georgia 7 days a week. Call (404) 632-9165 — Tony typically responds within minutes and can usually give you an honest same-day ETA on the spot.
