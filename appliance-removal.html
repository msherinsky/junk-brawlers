Got a question about appliance removal? You’re not the first.

Everything you were about to Google answered here.

01 Do you remove refrigerators that contain Freon or refrigerant? +
Yes. Tony handles refrigerant-containing units correctly, he doesn’t just drag them to the curb or dump them illegally. Freon disposal follows EPA guidelines. You don’t need to find a certified hauler separately or worry about environmental fines.
02 Can you pick up just a single appliance? +
Yes. Single-item pickups are common, one fridge, one washer, one dryer. There’s no minimum. A single appliance is usually a quarter to half load. Pricing starts at $125 and Tony gives you the exact number on the call.
03 Do I need to disconnect the appliance before you arrive? +
No. Tony disconnects it as part of the job, washer hoses, dryer vents, refrigerator water lines, gas range connections, whatever’s hooked up. You don’t need a plumber or electrician first. Just let him know what’s connected when you call.
04 What appliances do you take? +
Refrigerators, chest freezers, washing machines, dryers, dishwashers, ovens and ranges, microwaves, and wine coolers. If it plugs in or hooks up to a water line and you need it gone, Tony can almost certainly take it. Call and he’ll confirm anything unusual.
05 How much does appliance removal cost? +
Pricing is by volume, how much space your items fill in the truck. A single appliance is typically a quarter to half load ($125–$250). A full kitchen’s worth of appliances can be three-quarters to a full load ($375–$500). Tony gives you a firm price on the call before he comes out.
06 Do I need to be home when you pick up the appliance? +
For most pickups, yes, Tony needs access to wherever the appliance is located. If it’s in a garage or on a covered porch and you can leave it accessible, he can sometimes work around your schedule. Call (404) 632-9165 and he’ll tell you what’s possible for your situation.
