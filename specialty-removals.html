Specialty Removal FAQ

How much does hot tub removal cost? +
Hot tub removal typically runs $200–$400 depending on size, location on the property, and how easy it is to access. Tony gives you a firm price before any work starts, and that price doesn’t change when the job is done. Call (404) 632-9165 for a free on-site estimate.
Can you remove an above-ground pool? +
Yes. Tony and the crew handle full above-ground pool teardowns, draining, disassembly, and hauling away every piece. Call or text to describe your setup and get a quote. In-ground pools are not a current service.
Do you handle refrigerant disposal for appliances? +
Yes. Tony handles appliances with refrigerants, refrigerators, freezers, air conditioners, in accordance with EPA regulations. You don’t need to worry about proper disposal. It’s handled correctly every time.
What does shed or deck removal include? +
Full teardown and haul-away. Tony’s crew dismantles the structure, loads every piece of lumber, roofing material, and hardware, and leaves the area clean. No debris left on site. Price is set before work starts.
Do you remove construction debris from job sites? +
Yes. Drywall, lumber, roofing materials, concrete rubble, tile, and general renovation waste. Tony works with homeowners doing DIY renovations and contractors who need a job site cleared. Call in the morning and the crew can often be there the same day.
