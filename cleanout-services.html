Still have a question? You’re not the first.

The things families actually ask before booking one of these.

01 What happens to things the family still wants? +
Nothing leaves until you confirm it. The crew stages anything that looks personal, paperwork, photographs, jewelry boxes, and sets it aside for you to look at before the truck is loaded.
02 Do we have to be there the whole time? +
No. Plenty of these jobs are booked by someone out of state. Walk the property with Tony at the start, mark what stays, and he handles the rest and sends photos when it is clear.
03 How long does a full house take? +
Most are a single day. A packed property, or one where every room has to be worked through slowly, runs into a second day. Tony tells you which one it is after walking it, not partway through.
04 Do you sell or donate what comes out? +
Anything usable goes to donation or recycling before it goes to a landfill. If something has real resale value, you hear about it rather than losing it on the truck.
05 Is a hoarding cleanout handled differently? +
Slower, and without commentary. The crew works room by room at whatever pace the household can handle. Nobody is made to feel bad about the state of the house.
