Still have a question? You’re not the first.

Which job you have, and how a whole property gets priced.

01 Estate cleanout or hoarder cleanout, which one do I have? +
An estate cleanout is a house nobody lives in any more, usually on a realtor or probate clock. A hoarder cleanout is a house someone still lives in, where the pace matters more than the deadline. If it is both, say so on the call and Tony books it as the slower one.
02 Is a garage or an office cleanout the same job as a whole house? +
Same crew, same rates, smaller scope. A garage is usually one trip and one afternoon. A whole property is rarely one trip. Those two have their own pages because the walkthrough and the scheduling work differently.
03 Can one job be more than one of these? +
Often. A parent’s house that also got away from them is an estate cleanout and a hoarding job at once, and a property clearing can end with a garage nobody has opened in years. It gets booked as one job and quoted as one number.
04 Is a whole property priced differently than a single pickup? +
Same volume-based rates, more trips. The truck menu on this page is per trip. Tony walks the property first and gives you the number for the whole job before anything is touched, rather than a fresh price every time the truck comes back.
05 We do not know how much is in there. Can you still quote it? +
Yes, and that is the normal case on a cleanout. You do not need an inventory or a list. The walkthrough is what produces the number, which is why the price gets quoted after Tony has seen the property and not over the phone.
