Construction Debris FAQ

How much does construction debris removal cost in North Georgia? +
Pricing depends on volume and material type. Heavy materials like concrete and roofing shingles cost more per cubic yard due to weight. Most single-load construction debris pickups run $200–$500. Junk Brawlers provides a free on-site quote. Call (404) 632-9165.
Do you work with contractors, or only homeowners? +
Both. Tony works with homeowners doing DIY renovations and with contractors who need a reliable debris hauler on their vendor list. We work around your crew's schedule and can accommodate recurring pickups for larger projects.
Do you haul asbestos or hazardous renovation materials? +
No. We do not haul asbestos, lead paint materials, or any hazardous substances. These require licensed remediation and specialized disposal. If you suspect asbestos in older materials, please have them tested before calling us — we'll be glad to haul everything else.
Can you haul concrete and heavy masonry? +
Yes. We haul concrete chunks, brick, and cinder blocks. Due to weight, heavy masonry is priced differently than lighter debris. Give us an idea of the quantity when you call so we can quote accurately and bring the right equipment.
