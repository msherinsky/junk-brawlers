The Newest Houses We Work In.

Most of what we clear across North Georgia is decades of accumulation. Holly Springs is the exception.

Move-In, Move-Out, and the First Upgrade

The subdivisions here are newer, Avery and the developments off the Canton border among them. A house built in the last fifteen or twenty years has not had time to accumulate the way an older one has.

So the work is different in kind. It is the furniture that came from the last house and never fitted this one. It is a move-out where a family is clearing what they will not pay to transport. It is the first round of replacements, an original builder-grade appliance or the carpet in the bonus room, rather than an attic nobody has opened since the seventies.

That usually makes these shorter, cleaner jobs, which is worth knowing because it means a lot of them genuinely can be same-day rather than booked a week out.
