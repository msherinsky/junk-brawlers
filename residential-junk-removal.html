Still have a question? You’re not the first.

Everything you were about to Google about clearing out a house.

01 What if I have several different things to get rid of at once? +
That is one job, not several. An appliance, a mattress and a shed go on the same truck on the same trip, and you get one price for the lot rather than a separate quote per item.
02 Can you take just one item, or is there a minimum? +
Yes, and no. One couch, one fridge, one hot tub. There is no minimum. You pay for the space it takes in the truck, not a flat call-out fee.
03 Is a garage cleanout different from a full house cleanout? +
Different scope, same crew. A garage is usually one trip and one afternoon. Clearing a whole property after a death or a hoarding situation runs longer and gets handled more slowly, and that work lives on the cleanout services page.
04 Do you go inside the house, or only the curb? +
Inside. Upstairs, basements, attics, back bedrooms. The carry is included in the quote rather than added to the invoice afterward.
05 Will the price change once you see it? +
No. The price quoted is the price paid. Tony prices the job before anything is loaded, and that number does not move in the driveway.
06 How fast can you get here? +
Same day or next day. Call before noon and Tony can usually be there that afternoon. Open seven days a week.
