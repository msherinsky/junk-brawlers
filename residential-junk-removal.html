Residential Junk Removal FAQ

What residential junk removal services does Junk Brawlers offer? +
Junk Brawlers handles garage cleanouts, estate cleanouts, hoarder cleanouts, appliance removal, hot tub removal, pool removal, shed and deck removal, basement cleanouts, storage unit cleanouts, yard waste removal, and general furniture and junk hauling. If it's on your property, we can haul it away.
How much does residential junk removal cost? +
Pricing depends on volume and job type. A single-item pickup typically starts around $75�$125. A full garage or estate cleanout runs $300�$600+ depending on the scope. Tony always gives you a free, no-obligation quote on-site before anything is touched � the price he quotes is the price you pay.
Is same-day residential junk removal available? +
Yes. Same-day service is available across most of North Georgia 7 days a week. Tony is known for 30-minute response times in the Dawsonville area. Call (404) 632-9165 and we'll do everything we can to get out to you today.
Do I need to move things to the curb or driveway? +
No. We do full-service removal � our crew comes inside, goes to the garage, basement, attic, wherever the junk is, and hauls it out themselves. You just point at what goes. You don't lift a thing.
Does Junk Brawlers donate items instead of throwing everything away? +
Yes. Before anything goes to a landfill, we sort through and pull out usable furniture, clothing, kitchenware, and household goods for donation to local North Georgia charities. Recyclables get recycled. Only what can't be reused gets dumped.
