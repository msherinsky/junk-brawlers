Still have a question? You’re not the first.

Everything you were about to Google about estate cleanouts.

01 How do you handle items that might have sentimental or monetary value? +
Before anything gets loaded, Tony walks the property with you or your representative. Items you want to keep get set aside first. If there’s any question about something’s value, he’ll flag it rather than assume. The goal is to make sure nothing meaningful gets removed by mistake.
02 Do you work with estate attorneys or real estate agents? +
Yes. If you can’t be on-site, Tony can coordinate with an estate attorney, real estate agent, or family representative. He knows how to work efficiently around listing timelines and estate settlement schedules without slowing things down.
03 How is estate cleanout pricing determined? +
Same as every job, by volume. The price is based on how much space the items take in the truck. You get a firm quote after seeing the property. No per-item fees, no per-room charges, no invoice surprises when the job is done.
04 How long does a full estate cleanout take? +
It depends on the size of the property and how much is there. A standard home cleanout can take a few hours. Larger properties or those with heavy items, multiple outbuildings, or tight access may take longer. Tony can give you a time estimate when he gives you the price quote.
05 Do I need to be present during the cleanout? +
Not necessarily. As long as Tony has access to the property and knows what to take, you can designate someone else to be there. In some cases arrangements can be made with a key or lockbox. Talk to Tony and he’ll work out what makes sense for your situation.
06 What do you do with items that can still be used? +
Tony donates usable items to local charities and donation centers before anything goes to the landfill. He also resells salvageable items on Facebook Marketplace when appropriate. The landfill is the last resort, not the first stop.
