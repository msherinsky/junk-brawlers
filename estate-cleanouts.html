Handled with Care & Respect

Estate cleanouts are some of the most sensitive jobs we do. We understand that when you call us, you're often navigating grief while also trying to prepare a property for sale, settle an estate, or simply deal with a lifetime of belongings. Tony and his crew approach every estate cleanout with patience, professionalism, and zero judgment.

We can clear the entire contents of a home — furniture, appliances, clothing, kitchen items, garage contents, outdoor furniture, and anything else — in one or two efficient visits. We work around your schedule and at your pace.

Estate Cleanout FAQ

How much does an estate cleanout cost in North Georgia? +
Estate cleanout pricing depends on the size of the home and the volume of items. Junk Brawlers always provides a free, no-obligation quote before any work begins. Call (404) 632-9165 or fill out our form for a fast estimate.
Do I need to be there during the estate cleanout? +
Ideally, yes — it helps to have someone present to identify items to keep. However, if you can pre-designate what stays vs. what goes, we can often work without you present. Call us to discuss your situation and we'll work something out.
Do you donate items from estate cleanouts? +
Yes. Before anything goes to a landfill, we sort through it. Usable furniture, clothing, kitchenware, and household items get donated to local North Georgia charities. We believe this is the right thing to do for the community and the environment.
How quickly can you do an estate cleanout? +
Most single-family estate cleanouts can be completed in one full day with our full crew. Larger or more complex properties may require two visits. We'll give you a realistic timeline during the quote.
