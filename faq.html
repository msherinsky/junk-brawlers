01 How much does junk removal cost? +
Tony prices by how much space your items fill in the truck — not by the item, not by the hour. A quarter load is $125. Half load is $250. Three-quarter load is $375. Full truck is $500. A couch or a couple of appliances is usually a half load. A garage cleanout fills more. He gives you a firm number before he touches anything — and that number doesn’t change once he’s at the door. Call for a free quote: (404) 632-9165
02 Will the price change when you show up? +
No. The quote you get on the call is the price you pay. Tony doesn’t do bait-and-switch pricing — no “it’s heavier than we thought” surcharges, no surprise disposal fees. Labor, loading, and disposal are all included. The only thing that changes the price is if you add more items after he arrives, and only with your agreement.
03 What’s included in the price? +
Everything. Tony and his crew load it, haul it, and dispose of it responsibly. Labor, transportation, and disposal fees are all built into the quote. There are no extra charges for heavy items, stairs, or multiple trips to the truck.
04 Can you come the same day? +
Usually, yes. If you call before noon, same-day is almost always available across the full service area. Tony texts before he heads out and gives you a realistic arrival window — not a four-hour “sometime between 8 and 5” situation. Call and he’ll tell you exactly where things stand: (404) 632-9165
05 Do you actually show up when you say you will? +
60 reviews. Zero no-shows. Tony texts before he drives to your location. If something changes, he calls — he doesn’t ghost. He knows a no-show costs more than the job.
06 Do you work weekends? +
Yes — 7 days a week, 7am to 6pm. Saturday and Sunday are regular work days. No weekend premium, no extra fee. Call any day of the week.
07 What do you take? +
Most things. Furniture, appliances, mattresses, electronics, yard debris, construction debris, hot tubs, sheds, decks, scrap metal, tires, paint, batteries, e-waste, and more. The short list of things he can’t take: biohazardous waste, asbestos, and large quantities of certain chemicals. Not sure if your item qualifies? Call and Tony will tell you straight: (404) 632-9165
08 Do you take appliances? +
Yes. Refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, dishwashers, stoves, microwaves — Tony disconnects it, carries it out, and handles disposal. Refrigerant is handled properly per EPA regulations.
09 Do you donate or recycle, or does everything go to the landfill? +
The landfill is the last resort. Tony donates usable furniture and household items to local charities and donation centers. He resells salvageable items on Facebook Marketplace and recycles metals, electronics, and recyclables before disposal. He disposes in accordance with local environmental laws — not just the cheapest option.
10 Who shows up — you or a crew? +
Tony’s on the job. He’s the owner and leads every removal with his crew — often his brother and a helper for bigger loads. The owner who answers the phone is the one running the work at your door. No dispatched strangers, no subcontractors, no surprises.
11 What areas do you serve? +
Tony is based in Dawsonville and covers 10+ North Georgia counties — Dawson, Cherokee, Forsyth, Hall, Gilmer, Fannin, Lumpkin, White, Pickens, and Gwinnett. That includes Canton, Cumming, Gainesville, Blue Ridge, Ellijay, Dahlonega, Woodstock, Suwanee, Flowery Branch, and more. See the full list at service areas. Not sure if he covers your street? Just call — he’ll tell you straight.
12 Is there a travel charge or fuel surcharge? +
No. Tony doesn’t charge extra for distance within his service area. The quote covers the job — not the mileage. You’re not paying a franchise markup for a truck that drove from Atlanta.
