Pricing & Process

How much does junk removal cost in North Georgia? +
Pricing depends on the volume and type of items. Most jobs start at $99. We always provide a free, no-obligation quote before any work begins — no surprises. Call (404) 632-9165 or fill out our quote form. We base pricing on truck space used.
Do you offer same-day junk removal? +
Yes! We're open 7 days a week, 7am–6pm. Tony is known for 30-minute response times in the Dawsonville area. Call us and we'll do everything possible to get out to you same day or the next morning.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept cash, credit cards, Venmo, Zelle, and CashApp. Payment is collected after the job is done and you're satisfied with the results.
What are your hours? +
Monday through Sunday, 7:00 AM to 6:00 PM. We do not charge extra for weekend jobs.

In most cases, yes — we prefer you to be present so we can confirm what to take and what to leave. However, for straightforward jobs like curb-side pickups, arrangements can be made. Call us to discuss your situation.

What We Haul

What types of junk do you remove? +
We remove furniture (sofas, beds, dressers, tables), mattresses, appliances (fridges, washers, dryers, stoves), hot tubs, above-ground pools, sheds, decks, yard debris, construction materials (drywall, lumber, tile, concrete), garage contents, basement contents, storage unit contents, estate items, hoarder home contents, e-waste (TVs, computers), and general junk.
What can't you haul away? +
We cannot haul hazardous materials, chemicals (paint, solvents, pesticides, motor oil), asbestos, biohazardous waste, medical waste, or propane tanks. If you're unsure, just ask — we'll let you know.
Can you remove a hot tub or above-ground pool? +
Yes — hot tub and pool removal are among our most popular specialty services. We break down and haul away hot tubs and above-ground pools of all sizes, including all components and decking. Call for a free estimate.
Do you remove sheds and decks? +
Yes. We demolish and fully remove wooden sheds, metal sheds, wood decks, and similar outdoor structures. We clean up all debris as part of the job.
Can you do a hoarder house cleanout? +
Yes. Hoarder cleanouts are handled with zero judgment, full respect, and maximum efficiency. We've helped families restore homes that had been accumulating items for years. Call us to discuss scope and we'll provide a free walkthrough estimate.
