01 How much does junk removal cost? +
We price by how much space your load fills in the truck, not by the item, not by the hour. A quarter truck is $150. Half truck is $300. Three-quarter truck is $450. Full truck is $600. A couch or a couple of appliances usually fills about half the truck. A garage cleanout fills more. He gives you a firm number before he touches anything, and that number doesn’t change once he’s at the door. Call for a price: (404) 632-9165
02 Will the price change when you show up? +
No. The quote you get on the call is the price you pay. We don’t do bait-and-switch pricing, no “it’s heavier than we thought” surcharges, no surprise disposal fees. Labor, loading, and disposal are all included. The only thing that changes the price is if you add more items after he arrives, and only with your agreement.
03 What’s included in the price? +
Everything. We load it, haul it, and dispose of it responsibly. Labor, transportation, and disposal fees are all built into the quote. There are no extra charges for heavy items, stairs, or multiple trips to the truck.
04 Can you come the same day? +
Usually, yes. If you call before noon, same-day is almost always available across the full service area. We text before we head out and give you a realistic arrival window, not a four-hour “sometime between 8 and 5” situation. Call and he’ll tell you exactly where things stand: (404) 632-9165
05 Do you actually show up when you say you will? +
67 reviews. Zero no-shows. We text before we drive to your location. If something changes, we call, we don’t ghost. A no-show costs more than the job.
06 Do you work weekends? +
Yes. The crew works all seven days, 7am to 6pm on weekdays and 9am to 6pm on weekends. Call any day and we can usually get you on the schedule fast, often same day.
07 What do you take? +
Most things. Furniture, appliances, mattresses, electronics, yard debris, construction debris, hot tubs, sheds, decks, scrap metal, tires, paint, batteries, e-waste, and more. The short list of things he can’t take: biohazardous waste, asbestos, and large quantities of certain chemicals. Not sure if your item qualifies? Call and we’ll tell you straight: (404) 632-9165
08 Do you take appliances? +
Yes. Refrigerators, freezers, washers, dryers, dishwashers, stoves, microwaves, We disconnect it, carry it out, and handle disposal. Refrigerant is handled properly per EPA regulations.
09 Do you donate or recycle, or does everything go to the landfill? +
The landfill is the last resort. We donate usable furniture and household items to local charities and donation centers. We resell salvageable items on Facebook Marketplace and recycles metals, electronics, and recyclables before disposal. He disposes in accordance with local environmental laws, not just the cheapest option.

The crew. Tony wrote the standard they run and trained every Brawler to it, which is why the reviews read the same whether he’s on the job or not. The same crew every time, never subcontractors, never rotating strangers. He still answers the phone and gives you the price.

Home base is Dawsonville. We cover 10+ North Georgia counties, Dawson, Cherokee, Forsyth, Hall, Gilmer, Fannin, Lumpkin, White, Pickens, and Gwinnett. That includes Canton, Cumming, Gainesville, Blue Ridge, Ellijay, Dahlonega, Woodstock, Suwanee, Flowery Branch, and more. See the full list at service areas. Not sure if he covers your street? Just call, he’ll tell you straight.
