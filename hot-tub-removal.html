How Hot Tub Removal Works

Hot tubs are heavy, awkward, and usually stuck in the most inconvenient spot on your property — tucked against a fence, surrounded by decking, or wedged next to a structure. Junk Brawlers has the crew and the know-how to remove hot tubs from any location.

We don't just tip it and drag it — we break it down on-site. Most hot tubs get cut into sections using the right tools, then every piece is loaded onto our truck. When we leave, your yard is clean and the hot tub is gone for good.

The process typically takes 2–4 hours depending on the size of the hot tub, its location, and whether there's decking or fencing involved. Tony assesses the job on arrival and gives you the final price before any work starts.

Hot Tub Removal FAQ

How much does hot tub removal cost in North Georgia? +
Pricing depends on the size of the hot tub, its location, and accessibility. Junk Brawlers always provides a free, no-obligation quote before starting any work. Call (404) 632-9165 for a fast estimate.
Do you disassemble the hot tub on-site? +
Yes. Hot tubs are too large and heavy to remove whole in most cases. We break them down on-site into manageable sections using the right tools, then load every piece onto our truck. The process usually takes 2–4 hours.
Can you remove a hot tub that's surrounded by decking? +
Yes — we deal with hot tubs in tight spots all the time. If decking is in the way, we can remove it as part of the job. Let us know the situation when you call and we'll plan accordingly.
Do you also remove above-ground pools? +
Yes — above-ground pool removal is a separate service we offer across North Georgia. Visit our Pool Removal page or call (404) 632-9165 for a quote.
