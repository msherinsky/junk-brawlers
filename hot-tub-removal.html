Straight Answers.

Everything you were about to Google.

01 Do I need to drain the hot tub before you come? +
No. Tony handles the draining. You don’t need to prep anything before he arrives — just tell him where it is. He comes with everything needed to drain and remove it in one visit.
02 Can you remove a hot tub that’s on a deck or in an enclosed space? +
Yes. Deck-mounted, fence-enclosed, and tight-access tubs are part of the job. Tony will walk the site on arrival and figure out the best removal path before anything moves. Protecting the structure around the tub is a priority, not an afterthought.
03 Will you damage my deck or yard getting it out? +
Tony knows where hot tub removals go wrong and works to prevent it. He breaks the unit down on-site rather than attempting to move it whole — that’s how decks stay intact. He’ll be upfront with you if anything about the access creates extra risk before work begins.
04 Do you disconnect the electrical before removing it? +
Tony handles the disconnect as part of the removal. You don’t need to schedule a separate electrician beforehand. If there are any specific electrical questions based on your setup, he’ll address them when he arrives.
