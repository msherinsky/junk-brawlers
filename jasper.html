The Hauling Isn’t the Hard Part Out Here. Deciding Is.

Pickens County properties hold more than town properties do. There is usually a building for it, and usually more than one generation put it there.

When It Is Family Land, Somebody Has to Say What Counts as Junk

A lot of the calls up here are for a building nobody has fully opened in years. A barn off the back of the property, a lean-to that filled one load at a time, a shop that stopped being a shop sometime in the nineties.

The reason it is still full is almost never the labor. It is that somebody has to go through it and decide, and when it is family land that decision sits heavier than it looks. There is scrap metal worth something and an appliance that is only scrap. There is a box of tools that mattered to a person, and there is forty years of things kept because there was room to keep them.

We work at whatever pace that takes. Nothing goes on the truck until you have said so, anything you want a second look at gets set aside instead of loaded, and what can be recycled or donated goes that way before the landfill. If you would rather not stand there for the whole of it, give us the rule and we will work to it.

Getting a Truck Down the Drive, and Doing It Without You There

A lot of the property around Tate and out along Sharp Mountain sits well back off the road. Long gravel drive, ground that goes soft after a week of rain, and a building a hundred yards back with nowhere to turn something large around.

That is the practical case against a container. A roll-off has to be set down somewhere firm and level, and then it has to sit there until somebody comes back for it. We bring a dump truck and a crew that carries, so the question is whether we can reach the property, not whether there is a pad to park a box on.

And plenty of the houses up here belong to people who are not up here. If you own a place in Pickens County and live somewhere else, you do not need to drive up for this. Tell us what is going, you get the price before we start, and it is handled without the round trip.
