Frequently Asked Questions

How much does junk removal cost in North Georgia? +
Pricing depends on volume and the type of items. Most residential loads run anywhere from $100 for a single item to $400–$600 for a full truck. Tony always gives you a free, no-obligation quote on-site before any work begins — what he quotes is what you pay. No games.
Do you offer same-day junk removal? +
Yes — same-day service is available in most areas. Tony is known for 30-minute response times in the Dawsonville area. We’re open 7 days a week, 7am–6pm. Call us and we’ll do everything we can to get out to you today.
What areas do you serve? +
We serve 23 cities across North Georgia — Dawsonville, Canton, Cumming, Gainesville, Dahlonega, Jasper, Ellijay, Blue Ridge, Cleveland, Woodstock, and many more. Not sure if we cover your area? Just call — we’ll let you know immediately.
What types of junk do you haul away? +
We remove furniture, mattresses, appliances, hot tubs, pools, yard debris, construction debris, sheds, decks, hoarder home contents, estate contents, storage unit contents, e-waste, and much more. We do not haul hazardous materials, chemicals, or biohazardous waste.
Is Junk Brawlers eco-friendly? +
Yes. Before anything goes to a landfill, we sort it. Usable items get donated to local North Georgia charities. Recyclables get recycled. The landfill is the last resort, not the first stop. We care about the communities we serve.
Can you remove a pool or hot tub? +
Absolutely — pool and hot tub removal are among our specialties. We break down and haul away above-ground pools, in-ground pool fill-ins, and hot tubs of all sizes. Call us to discuss your specific project and get a quote.
