Frequently Asked Questions

How much does junk removal cost in North Georgia? +
Pricing depends on the volume and type of items being removed. We always provide a free, no-obligation quote before any work begins — no surprises. Call (404) 632-9165 or fill out our quote form and Tony will get back to you fast.
Do you offer same-day junk removal? +
Yes! We're known for 30-minute response times in the Dawsonville area. We're open 7 days a week, 7am–6pm. Call us and we'll do everything we can to get out to you the same day.
What areas do you serve? +
We serve 20+ cities across North Georgia — Dawsonville, Canton, Cumming, Alpharetta, Gainesville, Dahlonega, Jasper, Ellijay, Blue Ridge, Cleveland, Woodstock, and more. If you're unsure whether we cover your area, just call and we'll let you know.
What types of junk do you haul away? +
We remove furniture, mattresses, appliances, hot tubs, pools, yard debris, construction debris, sheds, decks, hoarder home contents, estate contents, storage unit contents, e-waste, and much more. We don't haul hazardous materials, chemicals, or biohazardous waste.
Is Junk Brawlers eco-friendly? +
Yes. We sort everything before disposal. Usable items get donated to local North Georgia charities. Recyclables get recycled. The landfill is the last resort, not the first stop. We believe in responsible disposal for our community.
Can you remove a pool or hot tub? +
Absolutely — pool and hot tub removal are among our specialties. We break down and haul away above-ground pools and hot tubs of all sizes. Call us to discuss your specific project and get a quote.
