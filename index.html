Still Have a Question? You’re Not the First.

Everything you were about to Google.

1 How much does junk removal cost? +
We charge by volume, meaning how much space your stuff takes up in the truck. You get a price before anything moves, and that’s what you pay.
2 What kinds of junk do you take? +
Pretty much everything. Furniture, appliances, construction debris, electronics, yard waste, mattresses, hot tubs, pools and more. We also handle refrigerants and hazardous materials the right way. Full list on the services page.
3 How fast can you get here? +
Call in the morning and it usually happens the same day. We’ve shown up within 30 minutes of a call before. We’re available 7 days a week. Call and a Brawler picks up.
4 Are you licensed and insured? +
Yes. Junk Brawlers LLC is licensed and insured. If something gets damaged on your property, that’s on our policy, not your homeowner’s. We’re a registered Georgia business and you can look us up.
5 Do you donate or recycle before going to the landfill? +
Yes. We resell whatever we can on Facebook Marketplace first. What we can’t sell gets donated. What’s left gets recycled. The landfill is the last stop, not the first. We live here too.
