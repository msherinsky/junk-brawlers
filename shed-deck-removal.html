We Handle the Whole Job

Shed and deck removal isn't just about ripping boards off. It involves safely breaking down the structure, removing fasteners and hardware, dealing with rotted or compromised materials, and then hauling every last piece away. Most homeowners don't have the crew, the tools, or the truck to do it right.

Tony and his crew handle sheds of all sizes — from small metal garden sheds to large wooden outbuildings. For deck removal, we work carefully around your home's siding and foundation, removing attached decks without damage. Freestanding decks and pergolas are no problem either.

Need the contents of the shed cleared before we demo it? We can haul those too — one visit, everything gone. Just let us know when you call.

Shed & Deck Removal FAQ

How much does shed or deck removal cost in North Georgia? +
Shed removal typically runs $200–$500 depending on size and material. Deck removal varies by square footage and complexity — most residential decks run $300–$800. Large or complex structures may cost more. Junk Brawlers provides a free on-site quote. Call (404) 632-9165.
Do I need to pre-dismantle the shed or deck? +
No. Junk Brawlers handles everything — we bring the tools and do the deconstruction ourselves. Just make sure the structure is accessible and, if there's a shed, empty out anything you want to keep before we arrive.
Will you haul the shed contents too? +
Yes. If there's still stuff inside the shed, we can clear it out as part of the job. Just let us know when you call so we can factor in the additional volume for an accurate quote.
Can you remove an old deck that's attached to my house? +
Yes. Attached deck removal requires extra care around ledger boards, flashing, and siding. Tony and his crew work carefully to remove the deck without damage to the house structure. We've done many attached deck removals across North Georgia.
