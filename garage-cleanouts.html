Still have a question? You’re not the first.

Everything you were about to Google about garage cleanouts.

01 Do I need to sort or bag anything before you come? +
No. Tony’s crew loads from wherever things sit. You don’t need to sort, bag, or move anything to one side first. Just walk through the garage with Tony, point out what you want to keep, and they handle the rest.
02 Can you take hazardous materials from a garage? +
Tony doesn’t take paint cans, propane tanks, gasoline, or chemical containers. Most other garage items, oil-stained tools, old batteries, electronics, automotive parts, are fine. If you’re unsure about something specific, ask on the call and he’ll tell you straight before he comes out.
03 How much does a garage cleanout cost? +
Pricing is by volume, how much space your items fill in the truck. A partially full single-car garage is usually a half to three-quarter load ($250–$375). A packed two-car garage can fill the truck ($500). Tony gives you a firm number after a quick walkthrough before anything gets loaded.
04 Do you take tools and yard equipment? +
Yes. Power tools, hand tools, lawn mowers, blowers, trimmers, garden equipment, all of it. If it fits in the truck, Tony takes it. That’s what makes a garage cleanout different from a single-item pickup, everything goes in one trip.
05 Can you remove appliances stored in the garage? +
Yes. Old fridges, chest freezers, washers, dryers, Tony handles appliance removal as part of the garage cleanout. No need to schedule a separate pickup. He handles refrigerant disposal correctly on units that require it.
06 How long does a garage cleanout take? +
Most single-car garage cleanouts take an hour or two. A packed two-car garage with heavy items can take a bit longer. Tony can give you a time estimate when he gives you the price quote after the walkthrough. Call before noon and same-day is usually available at (404) 632-9165.
