Everything you were about to Google about junk removal.

Answered straight.

01 What items can’t you take? +
We can’t take hazardous materials, paint, chemicals, propane tanks, bio-waste, or asbestos. Everything else is fair game: furniture, appliances, mattresses, electronics, construction debris, yard waste, hot tubs, sheds, and more. If you’re not sure, call and ask. He’ll tell you straight.
02 How do you figure out the price? +
Pricing is based on how much space your items fill in the truck, not by the hour, not by the item. You get that number on the call, and it doesn’t move. You get a firm number on the call. He doesn’t move on it when he arrives. As one customer put it: “His quote was very fair and they were efficient with removal and haul away.”, Darren Sorrells
03 Do you give an estimate before you start loading? +
Yes. We walk the job first, confirm the scope, and give you a firm quote before a single item gets touched. You approve the number. Then the work starts. No surprises on the bill when it’s done.
04 Can you come out the same day I call? +
Same-day is often available, especially for morning calls. Tony runs crews 7 days a week. Call (404) 632-9165 to check what’s open. He picks up himself and gives you a straight answer on availability.
05 Do you recycle or donate before sending things to the landfill? +
Yes. We donate usable furniture and household items to local charities first. Quality pieces get resold on Facebook Marketplace. Metals and electronics get recycled. The landfill is the last stop, not the first.
06 Do I need to be home for the removal? +
Yes, We do a walkthrough before loading and you confirm everything’s out before you pay. We’ll give you an ETA by phone so you’re not stuck waiting around. If your schedule is tight, mention it when you call and he’ll work with you.
