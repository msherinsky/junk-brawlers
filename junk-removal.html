Junk Removal FAQ

How much does junk removal cost in North Georgia? +
Pricing depends on volume — how much space your junk takes up in our truck. Most single-item pickups start around $75–$125. Full truckloads typically run $300–$500 depending on material type. Junk Brawlers always provides a free, no-obligation quote before we begin. Call (404) 632-9165 or fill out our quote form.
How quickly can you pick up my junk? +
Tony offers same-day and next-day junk removal 7 days a week across North Georgia. He typically responds to quote requests within minutes. If you need something gone fast, don't hesitate to call directly.
What items do you not haul? +
We do not haul hazardous materials, chemicals, asbestos, biohazardous waste, medical waste, or propane tanks (if full). If you're unsure whether we can take something, just call us and ask — we'll give you a straight answer.
Do you donate items instead of dumping everything? +
Yes. Before anything goes to a landfill, we sort through and pull out usable furniture, clothing, kitchenware, and household items for donation to local North Georgia charities. We recycle what we can. Only what's truly unusable gets dumped.
