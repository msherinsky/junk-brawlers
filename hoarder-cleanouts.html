Common questions about hoarding cleanouts.

Answered before you have to ask.

01 How do you handle items the resident wants to keep? +
Tony doesn’t decide what goes, the resident or family does. Before anything moves, he does a walkthrough to understand what’s staying and what’s going. Items the resident wants to keep are set aside and not touched. Nothing leaves without clear agreement on what’s being removed.
02 Can a family member hire you without the resident being present? +
Yes, in situations where a family member is managing the home, such as an adult child helping an elderly parent, or someone acting with power of attorney. Tony handles these situations regularly. Call (404) 632-9165 to discuss your specific situation and he’ll tell you what he needs to proceed.
03 Do you work with social workers or elder care professionals? +
Yes. Tony is comfortable coordinating with social workers, elder care case managers, and other professionals involved in the home. If you need him to work alongside or communicate with a care team, just let him know when you call.
04 Is the process confidential? +
Yes. Tony doesn’t discuss clients or jobs, not with neighbors, not on social media, not anywhere. The job is between him and whoever hired him. If you have concerns about privacy, call (404) 632-9165 and he can talk through them directly.
05 How long does a hoarder cleanout take? +
It depends on the size of the home and how much is being removed. A single-room situation can be done in a day. A full home with years of accumulation may take multiple visits over several days. Tony gives you a realistic timeline after the initial walkthrough, he won’t rush a job that needs to be done carefully.
06 How much does a hoarder cleanout cost? +
Pricing is based on how much material fills the truck per trip, using the same volume-based tiers Tony uses for all his jobs ($150–$600 per load). Larger situations often require multiple trips. Tony gives you a per-trip estimate after walking through the home so you know the full scope before any work begins. Call (404) 632-9165 for a price.
