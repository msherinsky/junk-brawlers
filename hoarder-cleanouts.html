Compassionate. Professional. No Judgment.

Hoarding situations are some of the most sensitive cleanouts we do — often involving a family member's lifelong accumulation, an elderly parent's home, or an inherited property that hasn't been touched in years. Tony and his crew approach every hoarder cleanout with patience, professionalism, and total discretion.

We work methodically — room by room — at a pace that respects the situation. Before anything leaves the property, we make sure the property owner or family has had the opportunity to identify anything they want to keep. We sort through for donation-worthy items and recycle what we can before anything hits the landfill.

These jobs often require multiple crew members and multiple truck loads over one or two days. We'll give you an honest assessment of the scope and a clear quote before we begin.

Hoarder Cleanout FAQ

How much does a hoarder cleanout cost in North Georgia? +
Hoarder cleanouts vary significantly based on the size of the home, the volume of items, and the number of truck loads required. These jobs typically run $500–$2,000+ for a full home. Junk Brawlers always provides a free on-site assessment and clear quote before starting. No surprises.
How long does a hoarder cleanout take? +
Most full hoarder cleanouts take one to two full days with a crew of 2–3. Some very large or complex properties may take three days. We'll give you a realistic timeline during our walkthrough so you can plan accordingly.
Does the person living there have to be present? +
It helps to have someone who can identify anything that should be kept. This is especially important in hoarder cleanouts to avoid accidentally removing something valuable or sentimental. If the primary resident can't be present, a family member or authorized representative works well.
Is the process confidential? +
Completely. Tony and his crew are professional and discreet. We don't gossip, we don't take photos for social media without explicit permission, and we treat every home — and every family — with the same respect regardless of the situation.
