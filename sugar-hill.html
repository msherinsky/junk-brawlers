New Builds Leave Their Own Mess

Sugar Hill has kept building, and new construction generates a specific kind of work that older towns do not.

There is the build itself: offcuts, packaging, broken board and the pallets everything arrived on. Then there is the move-in, where the new owner discovers the crate a range came in and forty boxes of cardboard. And then, a few years later, the trade-up, where a family moves within the same town to a bigger house and finally deals with the garage they never unpacked.

All three are volume rather than weight, which is exactly what a dump truck is for. Builders and agents can get a number in writing to hand to whoever is paying.
